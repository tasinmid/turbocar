Frequently Asked Questions

What documents do I need to rent a car?

You need a valid driving license (Bahrain or international), a passport or national ID, and a credit/debit card for the security deposit. For tourists, an international driving permit is required.

What is the minimum age to rent a car?

The minimum age to rent a car is 21 years. Some premium vehicles may require the driver to be at least 25 years old. A young driver surcharge may apply for drivers under 25.

Is insurance included in the rental price?

Yes, all our rentals include comprehensive insurance coverage. This includes collision damage waiver (CDW) and theft protection. Excess reduction options are available for additional peace of mind.

Can I cancel my reservation?

Yes, you can cancel your reservation free of charge up to 24 hours before the pickup time. Cancellations made within 24 hours may incur a fee.

Do you offer airport pickup and drop-off?

Yes, we offer complimentary pickup and drop-off service at Bahrain International Airport. Simply provide your flight details when making the reservation.

What is the fuel policy?

Our rental rates include a full tank of fuel. You can return the car with a full tank to avoid refueling charges, or we can bill you for the fuel used at competitive rates.
